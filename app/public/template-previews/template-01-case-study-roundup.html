MetaHRise

VR Training Client — MCMC

MCMC's induction used to be a slide deck and a printed handbook — forgettable by the second slide. We rebuilt it as a VR game new hires actually finish, learning the organisation by moving through it instead of sitting through it. It's the reason MetaHRise now holds a Malaysia Book of Records entry, not the other way around.

93% satisfaction · Malaysia Book of Records 2024
02 / 12

Say Hello, Tiger

Realtime AI Persona Client — Maybank Championship

Mascots are usually a photo op — wave, pose, move on. The brief asked for one that could actually talk, so we gave Tiger a realtime voice and a face that performs in sync with it. What's in the recap isn't a demo-reel moment; it's an unscripted exchange, captured live on the course.

Live voice + facial performance, on-ground
CHERITAR.EXE
03 / 12

CheritAR

AR Heritage Client — Think City

Think City wanted heritage buildings to speak for themselves without another plaque nobody reads. We layered the stories into AR instead — point a phone at a building and the history behind it surfaces on the spot. It's since travelled further than the brief asked for: the same trail has been picked up in twelve countries.

12 countries reached · 1,442 AR impressions
ARFESTKL.EXE
04 / 12

ARFestKL

AR Event Client — ARFestKL

A festival footprint only means something if people actually walk it. ARFestKL needed Downtown KL turned into a trail dense enough to survive a full weekend of foot traffic, so we built a QR-to-AR funnel engineered to take the volume without breaking. It held — over fifty thousand impressions and twelve thousand scans in two days.

52,417 AR impressions · 12,040 QR scans
IKAT.EXE
05 / 12

IKAT Malaysia

Immersive Exhibition Client — Ministry of Textile

Ikat weaving is generations of technique that doesn't survive well behind glass. The Ministry of Textile wanted people to feel the craft, not just read a wall label about it, so we staged the history as a full immersive environment instead of a static display. It pulled RM3.2 million in media value and over 5,800 visitors through the doors.

RM3.2M media value · 5,822 visitors
