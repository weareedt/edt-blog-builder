Every one of these started as an empty room, wall, or street corner, and a client asking what could actually happen there. Click through — each frame settles into focus as it comes to centre, the way walking up to it would.

CheritAR turns a heritage wall into a narrator instead of a plaque. IKAT Malaysia staged generations of weaving technique as something you walk through, not read about. New Balance's Grey Day put the fitting room inside the product shot. Malaysia Airlines' hologram stewardess covers a counter that would otherwise need staffing around the clock. And Tokyo Fire Department's VR drill rehearses a disaster nobody can schedule.

None of these rooms looked the same going in. That's usually the point — the room tells you what it needs before the technology does.
